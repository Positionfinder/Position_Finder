You will find information about available vacancies to form part of our teaching and research team below.

UIC Barcelona's Teaching and Research Staff is made up of professionals from the academic and research fields. Apart from teaching, UIC Barcelona teaching staff work on lines of research that reflect their professional field, and which add value to the classes they teach every day. 

The selection process is in line with what is set out under the HR Excellence in Research Seal (HRS4R) which UIC Barcelona obtained in May 2018. This guarantees an open, transparent and merit-based selection process, ensuring that there is a consistent focus on attracting the best talent.
