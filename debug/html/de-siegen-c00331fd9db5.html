MINToringSi - Studierende begleiten Schüler:innen auf ihrem Weg in ein MINT-Studium

Overview

MINToringSi is a cooperative program of the University of Siegen, the Arnsberg district government and the Association of Siegerland Metal Industry (VdSM). The aim is to get young people interested in STEM subjects. MINToringSi was offered for the first time in 2013 as a regional successor to the "MINToring® - Studierende begleiten Schülerinnen und Schüler" program of the Stiftung der Deutschen Wirtschaft (sdw).

The program involves supporting pupils interested in STEM subjects over a period of three years, starting in Q1.

MINToringSi

Program details

The first year of support familiarizes participants with the STEM field. This includes meetings, excursions, company visits and at least one STEM camp. Participants are familiarized with facilities at the University of Siegen.

The second year of the project focuses on STEM project work. They can practice scientific work in a team and acquire important skills for everyday STEM studies. The in-depth insights provided help to concretize study and career prospects. The work includes a practical research component, written documentation of the results and an oral presentation.

In the third year of support, workshops are held on topics such as learning and knowledge management, time planning and effective working in STEM studies. In addition, regular meetings are held for the purpose of exchanging ideas and experiences.

Goals

- "MINToringSi" strengthens the interest of upper secondary school students in Siegen-Wittgenstein in the subject of STEM,

- "MINToringSi" motivates students to take up STEM studies or dual STEM studies, especially at the University of Siegen

- "MINToringSi" stabilizes students in STEM studies and counteracts dropouts,

- "MINToringSi" promotes academic STEM jobs at VdSM member companies.

Advantages for students

- Intensive contacts in the network with like-minded people, students, the University of Siegen and local companies in the M+E industry,

- Dealing with STEM topics under expert guidance,

- Teaching key skills (e.g. through STEM camps and workshops) for your studies,

- Advice and support in the first semesters of STEM studies,

- Optimization strategies and motivation for further studies.

The application deadline is always September 30th of each year. Information on the application criteria and the application process can be found on the program's website
