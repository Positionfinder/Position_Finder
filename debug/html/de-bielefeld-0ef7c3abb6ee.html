Working well together. Bielefeld University as an employer.

Bielefeld University is an attractive, family-friendly place to work, characterised by an open culture of communication, interdisciplinarity, diversity and the freedom for personal development. In numerous different appointments, we work together to make an important contribution to a progressive and participatory knowledge society. We do this with a willingness to cooperate, full appreciation for the work of our employees and with great confidence in the abilities and academic achievements of each individual.
