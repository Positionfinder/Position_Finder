Organization Polish Academy of Sciences (IGSO PAS) was founded in 1953 (initially as the Institute of Geography PAS). The Institute is an important research centre in Poland in the fields of socio-economic geography, physical geography as well as spatial organization. Within the framework of the Institute, the Central Library of Geography and Environmental Protection is operating, this being one of the largest library centres of the kind in the world. ( more ...) Full text of the resolution 2026-09-21 35th Economic Forum - summary The 35th Economic Forum , held in Karpacz on 8-10 September 2026, was organised with the Institute of Geography and Spatial Organization of the Polish Academy of Sciences (IGSO PAS) as one of its partners. This year’s edition brought together more than 6,500 guests from 55 countries. Over the course of three days, representatives of politics, business, public administration, academia, social organisations and culture took part in debates devoted to the most important challenges of the modern world. The Forum’s central theme was The Architecture of a New Order – Stability in Times of Change . Representatives of IGSO PAS took part in debates devoted to key challenges related to the spatial and socio-economic development of Poland.
