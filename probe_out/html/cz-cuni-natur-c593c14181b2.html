Postdoctoral Hub

The Postdoctoral Hub is the go-to place for postdocs at Charles University, both for information and for meeting each other. Among other things, we run training workshops and a mentoring programme, offer microgrants, and share everything a postdoc at CU needs to know.


Who counts as a postdoc? According to Rector’s Directive No. 55/2025, a postdoc is an academic or research staff member of Charles University who obtained a PhD (or an equivalent from abroad) no more than eight years ago and has a fixed-term employment contract of at least 12 months. Parental leave and other legally recognised obstacles do not count towards the eight years. Postdocs are not only researchers who come from abroad: an assistant professor from Czechia can be one too.
