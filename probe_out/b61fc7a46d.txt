All of the following Information Systems are managed by the CZU staff - members of OIKT ( Division of Information and Communication Technologies ). UIS - University Information System is.czu.cz University Information System for CZU students and teachers. Here, students can mainly find: schedules, grades, registration for compulsory and optional subjects, sign up for exams, their personal information, requests for accommodation scholarships, paid scholarships and fees associated with studies, teaching evaluation forms and more. Moodle moodle.czu.cz Moodle is a university-wide e-learning system designed to support teaching. It includes courses for individual subjects and their study materials, forums, assignments, lectures, chats, polls... The Moodle system allows you to: browse individual courses containing information and materials from selected subjects, communicate with other students and teachers, hand in assignments online directly in the course, write tests and continuously verify your knowledge, and to always have an overview of the current academic year and follow current events and deadlines. UEW - University Electronic Wallet www.uep.czu.cz The information portal on the electronic wallet at CZU in Prague. Eduroam eduroam.czu.cz This website has information about the Eduroam project at CZU, which is implemented via the Wi-Fi network for connecting employees, students and guests from organizations taking part in the project. CZU library catalogue aleph.czu.cz An on-line overview of study literature. After logging-in, in the Reader section, the user can browse information about what they have borrowed – current and past, the status of the requests for what they want to borrow – and they can also extend the borrowing period and store found records in their inboxes. Helpdesk katedry.czu.cz/oikt/helpdesk The OIKT Helpdesk is a specialized department of the OIKT division that secures comprehensive user support for CZU in Prague. The OIKT Helpdesk department is an operating designation of a room which serves as a central point for providing services to users of the CZUacademic community. Personal websites of employees home.czu.cz Website enabling the creation of personal websites of CZU employees. Official department websites katedry.czu.cz Website enabling the creation of personal websites of CZU employees. A complete list of information systems and information websites can be found on the OIKT website .
